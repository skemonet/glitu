Steps and animations

Reveal content click by click with @n markers, choose entrance effects and highlights, set transitions and animate elements from one slide to the next.

Everything in a slide can appear on a click: a bullet, a paragraph, a word, a code window, a formula, or an element of a diagram. Clicks are numbered within each slide, and the same number means the same click everywhere in the slide.

Step markers

- Number of replicas @1                ← appears at click 1
- The ReplicaSet creates the pods @+1   ← one click after the previous step: click 2
- Visible from click 2 to 3 @2-3        ← then disappears
- Rolling update @4:up                  ← click 4, entering from below
MarkerMeaning
@nShown from click n
@+nShown n clicks after the previous step of the slide
@n-mShown from click n to click m, then hidden
@0-2Shown from the start, hidden after click 2
@n:effectShown at click n with an entrance effect

The markers work on bullets, ### titles, paragraphs, code windows (```yaml @1), formulas, words ([text]{@2}), images and videos (![](img/x.png){@2}), zones of an image ({focus="10 20 30 30 @2"}, see Focus) and skemo diagram elements (card#api API @2). The pauses of a video take one click each (see Videos). Keep the clicks of the text aligned with those of the visual: bullet @1 explains what appears at @1 in the diagram.

Effects

@2:effect chooses how an element appears: up, down, left, right, grow, shrink, zoom, red, green, blue or dim. Any fragment class of reveal.js works too.

Highlights

A highlight emphasizes an element that is already visible, for the duration of its clicks:

HighlightEffect
@3-4:haloA glow, once
@3-4:chaseA light running around the element
@3-4:magnifyThe view zooms on the element, then returns

halo and chase fade out after three seconds at most. A highlight is added to the entrance of the element:

- Persistent storage @1 @3:halo
kind: Service

The colour is the theme's accent, or --fx-color if your style.css sets it.

Blocks that replace each other

Several ### titles with a step in the same slide show one block at a time:

## Three ways to deploy

Transitions and backgrounds

Attributes on the ## line set the transition and the background of a slide:

## Results {transition="zoom" background="#123"}
## Our office {background="img/office.jpg"}

class="x" adds a class to the slide and zoom="0.8" scales its content. The theme sets the default transition in its glitu.yaml.

Zooming into a diagram

transition="dive:api" zooms from one slide to the next through a diagram: the diagram of the previous slide grows around its element api (a skemo card) until it fills the stage, and the diagram of the new slide comes out of it, while the text of the panel fades out, then the new one in. surface:api zooms back out to the general diagram; going back with ← plays either the other way.

## The API in detail {transition="dive:api"}
## Back to the whole {transition="surface:api"}

Instead of an element, a zone of the picture: dive:40 30 20 15 (x y width height, in %).

Animated slides

{.animate} on two consecutive slides animates what they share: elements with the same text, the same image or the same window title slide to their new place, and new elements fade in. To pair elements whose text differs, name them: [text]{#name}.

## Before {.animate}

- [Monolith]{#app}

## After {.animate}

- [Service A]{#app}
- Service B

Animated code

A ````md magic-move block (four backticks) holds several versions of the same code, one per click. The tokens that are kept slide to their new place, the others fade out or appear:

````md magic-move
```rust
let x = 1;
```
```rust
let x = compute();
```
````

See Code, diagrams and terminals for code windows and line highlighting.

Automatic advance and timing

data-autoslide on a slide, or autoSlide (in milliseconds) in the theme's glitu.yaml, advances the presentation on its own; A pauses and resumes it, and loop: true restarts at the end. Planned durations, written in slide titles (## Demo ⏳90), are described in Presenting.
