Layouts and media

Choose a slide layout with an attribute on its heading (columns, quote, key figure, cover, table of contents) and add images, videos, captions, focus zones, web pages and backgrounds.

By default, a slide has a text panel on the left and a visual on the right. An attribute on the ## line chooses another layout.

Layouts

LayoutWriteResult
Large visual## Topic {.large}The visual takes the full width; the bullets become a caption above it
Full## Topic {.full}The visual takes the whole slide under the header, without margins
Two columns## Topic {.cols}A ::right:: line separates the left column from the right one
Quote## Topic {.quote}The first paragraph in large type, the next one as its author
Key figure## Topic {.fact}The ### title very large, the text as its caption
Statement## Topic {.statement}The text in large type, centred; {.center} only centres it
Table of contents## Contents {.toc}The list of chapters, as links, generated
Title slide## Title {.cover} or {.intro}The title in large type, then a subtitle and a byline
Interlude## Part {.section}A section slide, without a number or a change of chapter
Closing slide## Thank you {.end}The final slide

Two more attributes adjust a slide: {kicker="Template"} replaces the label of its header and {.compact} tightens its bullets.

Images

![](img/architecture.png)

An image is the visual of its slide. To show it beside the text, covering one half of the slide, use {.image-right} or {.image-left}; the first image of the slide is used, or the one given with image=:

## Our office {.image-right image="img/office.jpg"}

### A team of twelve
- Remote first

Images are embedded in the built page. Files placed in public/ are copied as they are, which is useful for pages loaded in an iframe.

Attributes between braces, right after the image, set its click, a class, a caption or focus zones (see below):

![Office](img/office.jpg){@2 .framed}

box="x y w h" forces the place and size of a visual, in % of the area of the visual: after an image ({box="5 10 90 80"}) or on a block (```skemo box="5 10 90 80").

Captions and sources

A caption is a short line under the visual, typically to credit a source. On an image (or a video, or a terminal recording), write it as the title of the image, or with caption=:

![Population](img/population.png "Source: [INSEE](https://insee.fr), 2024")
![Population](img/population.png){caption="Source: INSEE, 2024"}

On a code window, a diagram or a formula, add caption="…" to the block:

```skemo caption="After the Kubernetes documentation"
diagram(dir="right")
  card#pod Pod
```

The caption is Markdown (links, emphasis), appears with its visual, and fits every layout: under the visual by default and in {.large}, {.cols} and {.full}, laid on the bottom of the image in {.image-left} and {.image-right}. Its colour and size follow the theme (.visual-caption in your style.css to change them).

Videos

A video is written like an image; the files go into img/ too:

![Demo](img/demo.mp4)
![Demo](img/demo.mp4 "Recorded on glitu 0.2"){autoplay muted loop}
AttributeEffect
pauses="0:12 1:05"Plays when shown, stops at each time; the next click resumes it
autoplayStarts when the slide is shown (without pauses)
muted, loopWithout sound, in a loop
nocontrolsHides the play bar
poster="img/x.jpg"The picture shown before the video starts
@2The video appears at click 2 (and starts then, with pauses)

mp4, webm and ogv files are shown as videos. A video loads only when its slide gets close, and stops when you leave the slide. Browsers may refuse to start a video with sound before the first key press or click on the page: add muted to a video that must start on the first slide.

Pausing at precise times

pauses turns a video into a series of clicks. The video plays as soon as it is shown, stops exactly at the first time, and waits; the next click plays it to the next time, and so on until the end:

## The rollout

### Watch the pods
- The new version starts @1
- The old pods stop @2

![](img/rollout.mp4){muted pauses="0:08 0:21"}

Here the video stops at 0:08 and 0:21; clicks 1 and 2 resume it, and the bullets marked @1 and @2 appear at the same time. Each pause takes one click of the slide, counted from the click where the video appears. Going back a click replays the previous part. Times are written in seconds (12, 12.5) or as m:ss and h:mm:ss.

Focus on part of an image or a video

focus="…" draws attention to zones of an image or a video: circle them, darken the rest, or zoom on them. A zone is written x y width height, as percentages of the picture measured from its top-left corner, followed by what happens there; zones are separated by ;:

![](img/dashboard.png){focus="62 8 30 20 @1; 5 55 40 35 .box .red @2; 70 70 20 20 @3:magnify"}
In a zoneEffect
nothingA hand-drawn circle
.circle, .box, .underline, .highlight, .strikeThe same hand-drawn marks as on words
.red, .green, .blue, .cyan, .yellow, .purpleThe colour of the mark (otherwise the theme's)
.spotThe rest of the picture darkens
@2, @2-3Shown at click 2, until click 3 (without a click: from the start)
@3:magnify, @3-4:magnifyThe view zooms on the zone, then returns
@3:halo, @3:chaseThe highlights of Steps

A zone that only zooms (@3:magnify alone) draws nothing. The zones stay on the picture whatever its size, including in {.image-left} and {.image-right}, where the image is cropped to fill its half: measure them on the whole image.

To find the coordinates, open the image in any editor that shows the cursor position, and divide by the width and the height of the image: a point at 620 × 80 px in a 1000 × 400 px image is at 62 20.

On a video

On a video, a zone can follow the clicks as on an image, or be tied to a time range of the video: 0:12-0:18 shows it from 0:12 to 0:18, 0:12 from 0:12 to the end, and magnify zooms on it during that time:

![](img/demo.mp4){muted pauses="0:12 0:30" focus="10 60 25 20 0:12-0:16; 55 10 40 40 .spot 0:30; 0 0 30 30 0:20-0:24 magnify"}

With pauses, a zone whose range starts at a pause is shown while the video waits there: here the circle appears when the video stops at 0:12 and leaves four seconds after the click.

Backgrounds

## Results {background="#0b1020"}
## The team {background="img/team.jpg"}

A background is a colour or an image, which covers the slide. The template themes also have an animated backdrop, shared by the whole presentation: see Themes.

Media and visibility

These attributes behave as in reveal.js:

AttributeEffect
data-autoplayStarts a video or a sound when the slide is shown (on a video written as an image: {autoplay})
data-srcLoads a medium only when its slide gets close
data-visibility="hidden"Hides the slide
data-state="x"Adds the class x to the page while the slide is shown
