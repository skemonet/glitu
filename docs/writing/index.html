Writing slides

The structure of a glitu presentation in Markdown: front matter, chapters, slides, text panels, speaker notes, formulas, icons, fonts and multi-file decks.

A presentation is a single Markdown file, usually slides.md. Images go into img/, terminal recordings into casts/, and larger decks can be split into several files.

Front matter

The front matter, between two --- lines at the top of the file, describes the presentation. It also produces the cover slide.

---
title: Kubernetes in practice      # cover title and page title
subtitle: Deploy and expose an application
author: Ada Lovelace
date: October 2026
description: A hands-on introduction to Kubernetes deployments   # for search engines
lang: en                           # language of the page
theme: crystal                     # see Themes
logo: img/logo.svg                 # in img/ or public/, or an https:// URL
navigation: 2d                     # one column per chapter (optional)
---
KeyEffect
title, subtitle, author, dateThe cover slide; title is also the title of the page
descriptionThe summary shown by search engines and link previews (defaults to subtitle)
langThe language of the page
themeThe theme, optionally at a version: crystal:0.1.0, or a local theme: ./themes/mine
logoA logo in the header (template themes) or in a corner (black, white)
global-top, global-bottomA mention on every slide, in Markdown: global-top: "© ACME"
font, font-title, font-monoFonts from Google Fonts, downloaded at build time
navigation: 2dTwo-dimensional navigation: one column per chapter
source: falseDo not embed the Markdown source in the built page
glituThe engine version the presentation requires, such as 0.1.0

Chapters and slides

# Resources                    ← chapter, numbered 01, 02…

Deploy, then expose            ← subtitle of the chapter slide

## Deploying an application    ← a slide; its header reads "RESOURCES / Deploying…"

### Deployment                 ← title of the text panel

- Number of replicas
- Rolling updates

```yaml title="deployment.yml"
replicas: 3
```
  • A level-1 heading starts a chapter and produces a chapter slide. # End {-} creates a chapter without a number.
  • A level-2 heading starts a slide. Its header shows the chapter and the slide title.
  • A level-3 heading is the title of the text panel, on the left.
  • The first visual of the slide (code, diagram, image, terminal, formula) goes on the right. A slide without a visual gives the full width to its text; a slide without text gives it to its visual.

Several ### Title @n headings in the same slide are blocks that replace each other, click after click.

Speaker notes

A paragraph starting with Note: holds the speaker notes. They appear in the speaker view (S), not on the slide.

Note: Start with the problem. [click] Then show the manifest. [click:3] Finally, the result.

[click] moves to the next click and [click:3] to click 3: the speaker view highlights the passage that matches the current click.

To getWrite
a word that appears on click[text]{@2}
a hand-drawn mark[word]{.circle @2}: .underline, .circle, .box, .highlight, .strike
a coloured markadd .red, .green, .blue, .cyan, .yellow or .purple
a CSS class[text]{.class}
an icon:mdi:github:: any Iconify icon, as collection:name

Marks are drawn when their click comes, or immediately without @n. Their colour defaults to the theme's (--mark). The spaces before : ; ! ? » become non-breaking, as French typography requires.

Formulas

Formulas are written in LaTeX and rendered as MathML when the presentation is built, so the page needs no script to display them.

The energy $E = mc^2$ inline, and as a block:

$$\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}$$

A ```math block is a formula used as the visual of the slide. In a formula written over several lines, keep = on the same line as a term: a line made of a single = is a Markdown heading underline.

Fonts and logos

font: Inter
font-title: Poppins
font-mono: Fira Code
logo: img/logo.svg
global-top: "© ACME 2026"

Google Fonts are downloaded at build time and embedded in the page. The logo must be in img/ or public/ (both are copied with the presentation) or be an https:// URL. global-top is shown in front of the content, bottom right; global-bottom behind it, bottom left.

Splitting a presentation into files

<!-- include: chapters/deployment.md -->

The line is replaced by the content of the file, relative to the file that includes it. The front matter of an included file is ignored, and its images stay relative to the main file.

The reveal-md syntax

A Markdown file written for reveal.js or reveal-md is recognized automatically and rendered with the black theme: slides separated by ---, vertical slides by ----, Note:, and the <!-- .slide: … --> and <!-- .element: … --> comments. See Themes.
