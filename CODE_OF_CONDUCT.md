# Code of conduct

glitu follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in the project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind and respectful, including towards differing opinions and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission;
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in every space of the project (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
someone represents the project in public.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, through the contact on the
[skemonet](https://github.com/skemonet) GitHub profile, or with GitHub's "Report content". Every
report is reviewed and handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainers may remove, edit or
reject contributions that do not follow this code, and temporarily or permanently ban a
contributor, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
